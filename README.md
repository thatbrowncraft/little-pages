# Little Pages 📖🤎

### Your little corner of thoughts.

Little Pages is a private, offline-first digital notes and journaling app inspired by the feeling of handwritten notebooks, old stationery, paper scraps, and cozy desk drawers.

It brings the simplicity of a notes app together with the charm of a personal digital journal.

## ✦ Features

### 📝 Notes

Create and organize notes for anything that matters to you.

Use Little Pages for:

- Personal notes
- Journal entries
- Quotes
- Writing ideas
- Study notes
- Lists
- Checklists
- Story ideas
- Random thoughts
- Everyday reminders

### 🔎 Search

Quickly search through your notes and find something you wrote without having to remember where you saved it.

### 🗂️ Folders & Tags

Keep different parts of your life organized with folders and tags.

Create your own system for:

- Writing
- Study
- Personal
- Journal
- Quotes
- Ideas
- Projects
- Anything else you need

### 📌 Pin & ❤️ Favourite

Keep important notes close by.

Pin notes that need quick access and favourite the ones you want to keep coming back to.

### 🗑️ Trash & Restore

Deleted notes aren't immediately lost.

Move notes to Trash and restore them when needed.

### ✒️ Custom Font Library

Make your notebook feel like yours.

Little Pages supports a personal font library where you can add custom fonts and use different fonts throughout your notes.

Individual notes can remember their own selected font.

### 📓 Notebook Feel

The interface is inspired by physical stationery and handwritten journals.

The visual design uses:

- Warm beige and cream tones
- Deep brown typography
- Paper-inspired surfaces
- Washi tape details
- Notebook textures
- Soft shadows
- Vintage stationery elements

The goal is to make opening your notes feel a little more like opening a favourite notebook.

## 🔐 Privacy

Little Pages is designed around local-first storage.

Your notes are stored locally on your device rather than being uploaded to a personal cloud account.

Your private notes are not part of the website's public content.

Because local storage belongs to the device and browser where the app is being used, different people using the same app installation do not automatically share the same notes.

> Your notes are stored locally on this device. They are not uploaded to a server.

## 🌙 Offline Use

Little Pages is designed to continue working when you are offline.

Once the application has been loaded and cached, you can continue using core note-taking features without an internet connection.

You can:

- Read notes
- Create notes
- Edit notes
- Search notes
- Organize notes
- Use custom fonts
- Favourite notes
- Pin notes
- Delete and restore notes

## 💾 Backup & Restore

Because your notes are stored locally, backups are important.

Little Pages provides JSON backup and restore functionality so you can create a copy of your notes and restore them when needed.

Backups can preserve your:

- Notes
- Folders
- Tags
- Preferences
- Custom fonts
- Note formatting
- Note metadata

Regularly exporting a backup is recommended for anything you don't want to lose.

> Local storage is not a substitute for backup. Export your notes regularly.

## 📱 Install as an App

Little Pages is designed as a Progressive Web App (PWA).

This means you can use it from a supported browser and install it on your device like an app.

On Android, open the application in Chrome and use the browser's **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** option when available.

Once installed, Little Pages can be opened directly from your home screen.

## 🌿 Design Philosophy

Little Pages is intentionally not designed to feel like a corporate productivity tool.

It's meant to feel personal.

A little notebook on your phone.

A place for the quote you don't want to forget, the scene idea that appeared at 2 AM, the grocery list you absolutely will forget, the study note you need tomorrow, or the random thought that somehow became important.

Some pages are useful.

Some are messy.

Some are completely unnecessary.

That's kind of the point.
